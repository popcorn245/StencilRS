# Roadmap

## Not ported yet (these fall back to Stencil's compiler)

- **Inline `styles` on scoped components**: needs a port of Stencil's `scopeCss`.
- **Per-mode styles** (`styleUrls: { ios, md }`) and component global styles.
- **Computed member names** and decorator arguments that are constants (`@Watch(CONST)`); these are
  reported as errors today rather than falling back.

## Missing output

- **The lazy-loading and SSR outputs** (Stencil's `loader-bundle` and `ssr` targets). stencilrs
  produces the customelement / `standalone` shape, which is what Vite, Vitest and Storybook use.
- **`newSpecPage` mode** (`@stencil/unplugin`'s `spec-page`).
- **Docs metadata**: JSDoc text and full `complexType` (resolved types and references) for
  `docs-json` and `components.d.ts`.

## Next

1. Inline `styles` on scoped components: a port of Stencil's `scopeCss`.
2. The lazy and SSR transforms (they share most of the customelement code).
3. A full `stencil build` replacement for the benchmark: stencilrs feeding Stencil's Rolldown
   pipeline in place of its TypeScript transform passes.

## Tracking

Each item above is an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/popcorn245/StencilRS/issues), with what
"done" means for it. Pick one up, or open a new one.
