### 6b. Regression note: the fold's rescan and its cold cost, found and fixed (`perf/fold-watch`)

`node --cpu-prof` of a watch rebuild and a trace of `scan` showed where the 90 ms went: not the runtime (it is transformed on every rebuild in the
unfolded build as well, and `foldBuildReads` on its 228 kB takes under 1 ms) but the scan's walk. The 344 specifiers that are aliases (`src/`,
`@utils/`, `@global/`) were each asked of the bundler (`ctx.resolve`) one after the other, about 0.65 ms each while Vite is busy, on every rebuild: 165 to 215 ms of
a 220 ms scan, which also held up the modules that import `@stencil/core/app-data` (their `resolveId` waited for the scan). On a cold build the same
serial walk took 450 ms of the 540 ms scan. A second cost, on every build: the compiler's `COMPILER_META` literal is never plain JSON (members with
no default are written as `"defaultValue": undefined`), so each of the 95 components fell back to `new Function` after a failed `JSON.parse` (about 16 ms of
main thread). Third, `scan.watch` listed each scanned file once per component that depends on it (4,665 `addWatchFile` calls for 367 files, 6 ms per rebuild).

Fixes (vite-fold.js, vite.js): the walk goes breadth first and asks the bundler for all the specifiers of a level at once; what it resolved is kept
across rebuilds (`resolvedCache`, flushed when a file appears in or disappears from the scanned folders, and per entry when the target is gone), so a rebuild
asks for nothing; `resolveLocal` is memoised per scan; the `undefined` members are dropped before `JSON.parse`; `scan.watch` is deduplicated;
`@stencil/core/app-data` is resolved at once and its `load` waits for the flags. The output is byte-identical (`diff -r` of `dist-stencilrs`).
Measured on this box (4 vCPU, load 0.8 to 1.7 from the other agent), fresh processes alternating (base = before, cur = after, off = `IONIC_FOLD=0`), 15 rounds, median (min-max), ms:

| Metric | before (fold on) | after (fold on) | `IONIC_FOLD=0` | Delta after vs off |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Edit one component, rebuild (`vite build --watch`, 30 rebuilds) | 378 (307 to 429) | 274 (235 to 321) | 258 (218 to 334) | +16 ms (+6%), was +120 (+47%) |
| Cold `vite build`, wall clock | 1,490 (1,382 to 1,677) | 1,424 (1,293 to 1,748) | 1,399 (1,286 to 1,604) | +25 ms (+1.8%), was +91 (+6.5%) |
| Cold `vite build`, vite's "built in" | 1,210 (1,110 to 1,400) | 1,170 (1,060 to 1,490) | 1,140 (1,030 to 1,300) | +30 ms, was +70 |
| Warm scan (`foldRuntimeFlags: 95 component(s) in ...`, verbose) | 220 | 22 to 37 | n/a | n/a |
| Cold scan, same line | 540 | 200 to 250 | n/a | n/a |

The numbers are from a different, noisier session than section 6 (the unfolded baseline is 258 ms here, 249 there), which is why the table carries its own
`off` column. What is left in a folded rebuild (+16 ms) is the walk's `readdir` and file stamps (about 10 ms standalone) running on the main thread beside Vite.
